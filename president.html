President's Message

A Word from the President

Welcome to TGU

Trinity Global University

The Trinity Global University was founded in the year of 2020 by the grace of God. I have been serving as the president of TGU since 2024 for training and equipping the church leaders and gospel workers around the world to accomplish the vision of world evangelization.

This task of mine as a president would be a great opportunity for me and also a big burden. The TGU ministry can be a good opportunity for the Korean missionaries in many countries and Korean churches to produce strong and healthy native church leaders by sharing mutual ministry experiences.

The TGU ministry team and professors are based on evangelical theology, profound pastoral experiences and overseas missionary careers, and they have passion and enthusiasm for soul winning.

Your prayers and support for TGU ministries to make faithful gospel ministers with burning heart for the world evangelization would be greatly appreciated. May God richly bless you.

Dr. Chun, Moo-Yup, Ph.D.President, Trinity Global University
