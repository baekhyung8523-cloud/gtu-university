What is TGU?

Trinity Global University was founded in 2020 by the grace of God for training and equipping the church leaders and gospel workers around the world to accomplish the vision of world evangelization.

TGU's ministry is a good opportunity for Korean missionaries in many countries and Korean churches to produce strong and healthy native church leaders by sharing mutual ministry experiences.
