What is TGU?

Our Beginning and Journey

TGU was established as a Korean branch of Chilchil International University (CCGU) of India, through a joint-degree program with CCGU.

The school began its early ministry as an institution that intensively trained doctoral students from universities across India. It educated not only the students sent by Indian universities but also missionaries serving around the world, providing retraining and doctoral studies. It also sought out respected missionaries who had served faithfully on the mission field for many years and awarded them honorary doctorates, in recognition of their encouragement, labor and devotion, and committed itself to more effective mission work.

Honorary doctorates were conferred jointly with the president of CCGU.

After the second president took office, the rapidly changing educational situation in India brought that ministry to an end. The school signed an MOU with Harvest University, which was already operating as an online school, and changed its name to Trinity Global Academy (T.G.A). It agreed to share all of Harvest University's existing systems (website, library, and more), and will operate as a tuition-free seminary that raises future leaders with spiritual depth from many nations.
